# Corvanta Technologies – Travel Policy (India)

Document ID: FIN-TR-IN-03
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Owner: Finance, Corvanta Technologies India Pvt Ltd
Applies to: Employees whose home country is India (Bengaluru office)

Employees based in Sri Lanka follow the Travel Policy (Sri Lanka). The policy of your home country applies wherever you travel.

---

## 1. Scope

This policy covers business travel by employees based in India: domestic travel within India and international travel to any other country, including Sri Lanka. It does not cover daily commuting or travel paid for by a client.

## 2. Definitions

- **Domestic travel**: travel within India that includes at least one overnight stay.
- **International travel**: any travel outside India.
- **Tier-1 cities**: Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai and Pune.
- **Travel desk**: the Finance team that books flights and hotels (traveldesk.in@corvanta.example).

## 3. Approvals

All travel must be approved in the **Travel** module of PeopleHub before any booking is made.

| Type of travel | Approvers (in order) | Submit at least |
|---|---|---|
| Same-day domestic travel | No approval needed | – |
| Domestic travel with overnight stay | Line manager | 5 working days before travel |
| International travel, grades L1–L4 | Line manager, then Department Head, then Finance Controller (India) | 10 working days before travel |
| International travel, grades L5–L6 | Department Head, then Finance Controller (India) | 10 working days before travel |

Travel booked without approval will not be reimbursed. The business days of the trip must also be recorded as On Duty – Travel (OD-T) in PeopleHub, as set out in the Leave Policy.

## 4. Booking

1. After approval, the travel desk books flights and hotels. Employees must not book directly.
2. For international travel, the employee is responsible for a valid passport and visa. Sri Lanka requires an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA); apply at least 7 days before travel.
3. The travel desk sends the itinerary by email at least 3 working days before departure.

## 5. Travel Class

| Grade | Domestic flights | International flights |
|---|---|---|
| L1–L4 | Economy | Economy |
| L5–L6 | Economy | Business class for flights over 6 hours; Economy otherwise |

Flights to Colombo are under 6 hours, so all grades travel Economy.

## 6. Accommodation

Maximum hotel rate per night, including taxes:

| Location | L1–L4 | L5–L6 |
|---|---|---|
| Tier-1 cities | INR 7,500 | INR 11,000 |
| Other Indian cities | INR 5,000 | INR 8,000 |
| International | USD 150 | USD 220 |

## 7. Daily Allowance

The daily allowance covers meals, local transport and incidentals. It is paid per day of travel, with half the rate for the day of return.

| Location | L1–L4 | L5–L6 |
|---|---|---|
| Tier-1 cities | INR 3,000 | INR 4,000 |
| Other Indian cities | INR 2,000 | INR 3,000 |
| International (including Sri Lanka) | USD 75 | USD 100 |

## 8. Travel Advances

- Advances are available for international travel only, up to 80% of the expected daily allowance.
- Request the advance in the same PeopleHub travel request.
- Foreign currency is issued as a forex card by the travel desk 2 working days before departure.

## 9. Claims and Reimbursement

1. Submit your expense claim in PeopleHub within **15 calendar days** of returning.
2. Attach receipts for every item above INR 500.
3. Settle any unused advance in the same claim.
4. The line manager approves the claim. Finance pays it with the next salary cycle.

Claims submitted after 15 days need Finance Controller (India) approval and may be rejected.

## 10. Combining Leave with Travel

Personal days added to a business trip are annual leave and follow the Leave Policy. The company pays only the costs it would have paid for the business days alone.
