# Corvanta Technologies – Travel Policy (Sri Lanka)

Document ID: FIN-TR-LK-02
Version: 2.1
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Owner: Finance, Corvanta Technologies Lanka (Pvt) Ltd
Applies to: Employees whose home country is Sri Lanka (Colombo office)

Employees based in India follow the Travel Policy (India). The policy of your home country applies wherever you travel.

---

## 1. Scope

This policy covers business travel by employees based in Sri Lanka: domestic travel within Sri Lanka and international travel to any other country, including India. It does not cover daily commuting or travel paid for by a client.

## 2. Definitions

- **Domestic travel**: travel within Sri Lanka that includes at least one overnight stay, or any same-day trip of more than 100 km each way.
- **International travel**: any travel outside Sri Lanka.
- **Colombo district**: all locations within the Colombo administrative district.
- **Travel desk**: the Finance team that books flights and hotels (traveldesk.lk@corvanta.example).

## 3. Approvals

All travel must be approved in the **Travel** module of PeopleHub before any booking is made.

| Type of travel | Approvers (in order) | Submit at least |
|---|---|---|
| Same-day travel under 100 km each way | No approval needed | – |
| Domestic travel (overnight or over 100 km) | Line manager | 3 working days before travel |
| International travel, all grades | Line manager, then Country Director (Sri Lanka), then Finance Manager (Sri Lanka) | 14 working days before travel |

Travel booked without approval will not be reimbursed. The business days of the trip must also be recorded as On Duty – Travel (OD-T) in PeopleHub, as set out in the Leave Policy.

## 4. Booking

1. After approval, the travel desk books flights and hotels. Employees must not book directly.
2. For international travel, the employee is responsible for a valid passport and visa. India requires an e-Visa for Sri Lankan citizens on business; apply at least 10 days before travel.
3. The travel desk sends the itinerary by email at least 3 working days before departure.

## 5. Travel Class

| Grade | Domestic travel | International flights |
|---|---|---|
| L1–L4 | Company car pool or rail (first class) | Economy |
| L5–L6 | Company car pool or rail (first class) | Business class for flights over 5 hours; Economy otherwise |

## 6. Accommodation

Maximum hotel rate per night, including taxes:

| Location | L1–L4 | L5–L6 |
|---|---|---|
| Colombo district | LKR 30,000 | LKR 45,000 |
| Outside Colombo district | LKR 22,000 | LKR 32,000 |
| International | USD 140 | USD 200 |

## 7. Daily Allowance

The daily allowance covers meals, local transport and incidentals. It is paid per day of travel, with half the rate for the day of return.

| Location | L1–L4 | L5–L6 |
|---|---|---|
| Colombo district | LKR 9,000 | LKR 12,000 |
| Outside Colombo district | LKR 6,500 | LKR 9,000 |
| International (including India) | USD 70 | USD 95 |

## 8. Travel Advances

- Advances are available for domestic and international travel, up to 100% of the expected daily allowance.
- Request the advance in the same PeopleHub travel request.
- Foreign currency is paid in USD cash or to a forex card 3 working days before departure.

## 9. Claims and Reimbursement

1. Submit your expense claim in PeopleHub within **10 working days** of returning.
2. Attach receipts for every item above LKR 2,000.
3. Settle any unused advance in the same claim.
4. The line manager approves the claim. Finance pays it within 7 working days of approval.

Claims submitted after 10 working days need Finance Manager (Sri Lanka) approval and may be rejected.

## 10. Combining Leave with Travel

Personal days added to a business trip are annual leave and follow the Leave Policy. The company pays only the costs it would have paid for the business days alone.
