## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains the types of leave available, how to apply, who approves, and how unused leave is carried forward. It applies to all permanent employees in India and Sri Lanka. Contract staff follow the terms in their contract.

Where local law gives an employee more leave than this policy, local law applies.

## 2. Summary of Changes from 2025

- The annual leave carry-forward limit increases from **10 days to 15 days**.
- Carried-forward leave must now be used by **30 June** of the following year (previously 31 March).
- A new section 7 clarifies how business travel is recorded and how leave can be combined with a business trip.

All other entitlements are unchanged from 2025.

## 3. Leave Entitlements

| Leave type | Days per calendar year | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Annual leave | 18 | Accrues at 1.5 days per month of service |
| Casual leave | 7 | For short personal needs; cannot be carried forward |
| Sick leave | 10 | Medical certificate needed for 3 or more consecutive days |
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks | As per local law; full pay |
| Paternity leave | 10 working days | To be taken within 3 months of birth or adoption |
| Bereavement leave | 5 working days | Immediate family |

Public holidays are published separately for each country by HR in December each year.

## 4. Applying for Leave

1. Log in to **PeopleHub** (connect to the VPN if you are outside the office).
2. Select **Leave → Apply**.
3. Choose the leave type and dates. PeopleHub shows your remaining balance.
4. Add a short reason and, for sick leave of 3 or more days, attach a medical certificate.
5. Submit. The request goes to your approvers listed in section 5.
6. Do not start planned leave until the request shows **Approved**.

Emergency or sick leave may be applied for after the absence, within 2 working days of returning to work.

## 5. Approvals

| Length of leave (working days) | Approvers | Minimum notice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 3 days | Line manager | 3 working days |
| 4 to 10 days | Line manager, then HR Business Partner | 2 weeks |
| More than 10 days | Line manager, then Department Head, then HR Business Partner | 4 weeks |

Approvers must respond within 2 working days. If the line manager is on leave, the request goes automatically to the manager's own line manager.

## 6. Carry-Forward of Annual Leave

- Up to **15 days** of unused annual leave can be carried forward into the next calendar year.
- Carried-forward days must be used by **30 June** of that year. Any carried-forward days not used by 30 June lapse.
- Annual leave above 15 days at 31 December lapses and cannot be encashed, except where local law requires encashment.
- Casual leave and sick leave cannot be carried forward.

Example: An employee has 19 days of unused annual leave on 31 December 2026. 15 days are carried into 2027 and must be used by 30 June 2027. The remaining 4 days lapse.

## 7. Business Travel and Leave

### 7.1 Business travel is not leave

Days spent on approved business travel are recorded in PeopleHub as **On Duty – Travel (OD-T)**. OD-T days do not reduce any leave balance. Do not apply for annual or casual leave for days you are travelling on company business.

To record OD-T, select **Attendance → On Duty → Travel** in PeopleHub. Your line manager approves the OD-T entry. The trip itself must also be approved under the travel policy for your home country (`travel-policy-india.md` or `travel-policy-sri-lanka.md`).

### 7.2 Combining leave with a business trip

If you add personal days before or after a business trip:

1. Record the business days as OD-T (section 7.1).
2. Apply for annual leave for the personal days, following the approvals in section 5.
3. Declare the personal days in your travel request so the travel desk can book correctly.
4. The company pays only the travel cost it would have paid for the business days alone. Extra hotel nights and fare differences are paid by the employee.

## 8. Leave Without Pay

Leave without pay may be granted only after all annual and casual leave is used. It needs approval from the line manager, Department Head and HR Business Partner, and cannot exceed 30 working days in a calendar year.
